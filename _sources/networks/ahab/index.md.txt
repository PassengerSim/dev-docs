# Ahab Network

Ahab is a very small network intended to support testing of the PassengerSim simulation engine. It
has just one directional market between two airports: Nantucket (ACK), Massachusetts, and Nadi
(NAN), Fiji. It is a fictional example, not a realistic representation of rational travel between
these locations. With only one long voyage and no connections or competing carriers, it is smaller
and simpler than any other test network, but it may be suitable for single-minded pursuits such as
hunting white whales (or software bugs).

**Pequod** (`Pequod`) is the network's only carrier. It operates one 100-seat voyage from ACK to
NAN, with no return trip. It has six fare classes, and baseline fares range from $1,000 to $4,000,
with advance-purchase requirements and restrictions that vary by fare class. Demand consists of
business and leisure travelers, with base demands of 40 and 60 passengers, respectively.

The baseline uses revenue management system `E` and runs one trial of 1,000 samples, discarding the
first 100 samples as burn-in. The network is included with PassengerSim and can be loaded with
`pax.Config.from_yaml(pax.demo_network("ahab/baseline"))`.
