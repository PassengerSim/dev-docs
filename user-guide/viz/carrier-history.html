    Carrier Histories¶

    Average revenue tells us part of the story. These charts let us look at the variation from one simulation sample to the next, and see whether good results for one carrier tend to coincide with good results for another.

    You can read about the underlying data and how to collect it in Carrier Histories.

    Both figures use carrier_history2, which is collected directly without database recording. Neither has a dedicated Contrast equivalent.

    Sample Revenue Distribution¶

    This chart shows a smoothed distribution of revenue outcomes over samples for each carrier. A wider shape means revenue varies more from sample to sample; a narrow shape means the results are more consistent.

    This figure uses the results collected in the carrier_history2 table. The raw_df option is not implemented for this figure, but you can access summary.carrier_history2 directly for the underlying observations.

    For one run, call fig_carrier_revenue_distribution on a single SimulationTables object:

    summary.fig_carrier_revenue_distribution()

    To compare scenarios, make this chart once for each result. There isn’t a combined comparison method for this figure.

    Comparing Revenue for Two Carriers¶

    The univariate distribution above shows each carrier’s revenue separately. This chart compares two selected carriers head-to-head, showing how their revenues vary together from sample to sample within a single simulation run. Each point represents one simulation sample, with the x-coordinate showing one carrier’s revenue and the y-coordinate showing the other’s.

    This lets you see whether the carriers tend to do well together, or whether a good result for one often comes with a weaker result for the other.

    Choose both x_carrier and y_carrier for a single head-to-head plot. With mean_adjusted=True (default), each axis is relative to that carrier’s mean; set it to False for revenue in currency units.

    For one run, call fig_carrier_head_to_head_revenue on a single SimulationTables object:

    summary.fig_carrier_head_to_head_revenue(x_carrier="Tapas", y_carrier="Stroop")

    Omit both carrier names to generate a scatter plot matrix for all carriers. Each row and column represents a carrier, and each panel pairs revenues from the same trial and sample. The red line marks equal revenue (or equal percentages of mean revenue when mean adjustment is enabled).

    Call fig_carrier_head_to_head_revenue on a single SimulationTables object without carrier arguments:

    summary.fig_carrier_head_to_head_revenue()

    To compare scenarios, make this chart once for each result. There isn’t a combined comparison method for this figure.
